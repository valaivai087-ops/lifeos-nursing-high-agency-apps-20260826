讓你痛苦的不是事情，是你怎麼看待它。
It's not the event that hurts you, but how you see it.

計畫失敗了，你說：「我完了。」
When a plan fails, you say, "I'm finished."
被拒絕了，你說：「我果然不行。」
When you're rejected, you say, "I knew I wasn't good enough."
犯了錯，你說：「我怎麼這麼笨。」
When you make a mistake, you say, "How could I be so stupid?"

親愛的，別急著給自己判刑。
Dear one, don't rush to sentence yourself.
同一件事，換個角度看：
Look at the same thing from another angle:
失敗，也許只是「這條路不適合我」；
Failure may simply mean "this path isn't for me";
拒絕，也許只是「時機還沒到」；
Rejection may simply mean "the timing isn't right yet";
犯錯，也許只是「我又學會了一件事」。
A mistake may simply mean "I've learned something new."

✨ 生命裡的每一件事，本來都沒有標籤，
✨ Nothing in life comes with a label;
✨ 是我們親手替它寫上了「悲劇」兩個字。
✨ It's we who write "tragedy" on it with our own hands.

同樣的雨，有人說是災難，有人說是滋潤；
The same rain is a disaster to some, nourishment to others;
同樣的夜，有人說是黑暗，有人說是星空；
The same night is darkness to some, a sky of stars to others;
同樣的跌倒，有人說是結束，有人說是開始。
The same fall is an ending to some, a beginning to others.

✨ 事情本身不會傷人，傷人的是我們給它的意義。
✨ Events don't wound us; the meanings we give them do.

下次想責怪自己的時候，先停十秒，
Next time you want to blame yourself, pause for ten seconds,
溫柔地問自己一句：
And gently ask yourself:
「還有沒有別的可能？」
"Could there be another way to see this?"

你不是失敗者，你只是還在學習的人。
You are not a failure; you are someone still learning.
✨ 換一個解釋，就換了一種心情；
✨ Change the story, and you change the feeling;
✨ 換一種心情，就換了一段人生。
✨ Change the feeling, and you change your life.
願你對自己，永遠比對世界更溫柔。☁️
May you always be gentler with yourself than with the world. ☁️
