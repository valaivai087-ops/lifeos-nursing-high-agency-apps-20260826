別人的眼光，不該是你人生的方向盤。
Other people's opinions should not be the steering wheel of your life.

你是不是也這樣——
Are you like this too —
穿什麼衣服，要先想別人怎麼看；
Before choosing clothes, you think about how others will see you;
說什麼話，要先想別人會不會不高興；
Before speaking, you wonder whether others will be upset;
做什麼決定，要先問別人怎麼想。
Before deciding anything, you ask what others think.

活了好久，才發現，
After many years, you finally realize
那些你拚命在意的眼光，
that the eyes you cared so desperately about
其實沒有人真的一直在看你。
were never really watching you all the time.

✨ 你活在別人的嘴裡，就會死在自己的心裡。
✨ If you live by other people's words, your own heart slowly dies.

人生很短，請把最重要的位置：
Life is short, so give the most important places
留給健康，
to your health,
留給家人，
to your family,
留給愛你的人，
to those who love you,
留給自己的成長。
and to your own growth.

✨ 別人怎麼說，是別人的事；
✨ What others say is their business;
✨ 你怎麼活，才是你的事。
✨ How you live is yours.

少一點比較，多一點專注；
Compare a little less, focus a little more;
少一點面子，多一點裡子；
Care less about appearances, more about substance;
少一點討好，多一點做自己。
Please others a little less, be yourself a little more.

✨ 當你不再需要所有人喜歡你，你才真正開始喜歡自己。
✨ When you no longer need everyone to like you, you truly begin to like yourself.
願你把人生的方向盤，穩穩握在自己手裡。🍃
May you hold the steering wheel of your life firmly in your own hands. 🍃
