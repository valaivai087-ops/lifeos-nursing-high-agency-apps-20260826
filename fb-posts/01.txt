你看見的世界，是你心的樣子。
The world you see is the shape of your heart.

有時候，我們覺得全世界都在跟自己作對。
Sometimes it feels like the whole world is against us.
同事的一句話，像在指責你；
A coworker's remark sounds like blame;
朋友沒回的訊息，像在冷落你；
A friend's unanswered message feels like rejection;
家人的一個眼神，像在否定你。
A family member's glance feels like disapproval.

可是親愛的，請你停下來想一想：
But, dear one, please pause and ask yourself:
那是真的發生了，還是你心裡害怕發生？
Did it really happen, or is it what you fear might happen?

我們看見的，常常不是世界本來的樣子；
What we see is often not the world as it is;
我們聽見的，常常不是別人真正的意思；
What we hear is often not what others truly mean;
我們感受到的，常常是過去那道傷口的回聲。
What we feel is often the echo of an old wound.

✨ 心若是灰的，晴天也會下雨；
✨ When the heart is grey, even sunny days bring rain;
✨ 心若是亮的，雨天也能看見光。
✨ When the heart is bright, even rainy days hold light.
✨ 你怎麼看世界，世界就怎麼回應你。
✨ The way you see the world is the way the world answers you.

所以，先寫下發生了什麼，再寫下你的感受；
So first write down what happened, then how you feel;
先看清楚事實，再決定要不要難過；
First see the facts clearly, then decide whether to be sad;
先深呼吸三次，再決定要不要生氣；
First take three deep breaths, then decide whether to be angry;
先問一句「真的是這樣嗎？」，再下結論。
First ask, "Is it really so?" — then draw a conclusion.

不是世界太冷，是你太久沒被溫柔對待；
It's not that the world is too cold — you just haven't been treated gently for too long;
不是別人太壞，是你心裡的傷還在痛。
It's not that others are cruel — your wound still aches.

✨ 換一雙溫柔的眼睛，世界就會換一種樣子。
✨ Look with gentler eyes, and the world will show a gentler face.
願你眼裡有光，心裡有暖；
May there be light in your eyes and warmth in your heart;
願你眼裡的世界，一天比一天溫柔。🌿
May the world in your eyes grow gentler day by day. 🌿
