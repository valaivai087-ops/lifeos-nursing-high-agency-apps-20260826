你不是不夠自律，你只是太累了。
You are not undisciplined — you are simply exhausted.

總是責怪自己不夠努力，
You always blame yourself for not trying hard enough,
總是責怪自己不夠自律，
always blame yourself for lacking discipline,
總是責怪自己不夠堅強，
always blame yourself for not being strong enough,
卻忘了問：我是不是好久沒有好好睡一覺了？
but you forget to ask: when did I last get a good night's sleep?

熬夜，會偷走你的耐心；
Staying up late steals your patience;
高糖，會偷走你的穩定；
Too much sugar steals your steadiness;
壓力，會偷走你的笑容。
Stress steals your smile.

✨ 再堅強的心，也需要一個被照顧好的身體。
✨ Even the strongest heart needs a body that's been cared for.

不是你不夠好，
It's not that you're not good enough;
是你的身體，已經撐了太久。
it's that your body has been holding on for too long.

善待自己的身體吧：
Be kind to your body:
早一點睡，
sleep a little earlier,
多喝一點水，
drink a little more water,
早上去曬十分鐘太陽，
get ten minutes of morning sunlight,
每天動一動，讓身體重新有力量。
move every day, so your body can find its strength again.

✨ 休息不是偷懶，是為了走更遠的路。
✨ Rest isn't laziness; it's how you go farther.
✨ 身體被照顧好了，心也會跟著亮起來。
✨ When your body is cared for, your heart lights up too.

今晚，早一點關燈，
Tonight, turn off the lights a little earlier,
對辛苦了一整天的自己說聲：
and say to yourself, who worked so hard all day:
「謝謝你，辛苦了。」
"Thank you. You did well."
願你每天醒來，都有滿滿的力氣。☀️
May you wake up every day full of strength. ☀️
