懂得很多道理，不如真正活出一個。
Knowing many truths is not as good as truly living one.

我們收藏了好多語錄，
We've saved so many quotes,
看了好多懶人包，
read so many quick summaries,
聽了好多人生道理，
heard so many life lessons,
可是日子，好像還是老樣子。
and yet our days seem just the same.

✨ 道理不是拿來收藏的，是拿來活的。
✨ Truths aren't for collecting; they're for living.

看過一百本書，不如認真讀懂一本；
Reading a hundred books is not as good as truly understanding one;
記住一百句話，不如真正做到一句；
Memorizing a hundred sayings is not as good as truly living one;
知道一百種方法，不如堅持用好一種。
Knowing a hundred methods is not as good as sticking with one.

✨ 知道是起點，做到才是終點。
✨ Knowing is the starting line; doing is the finish.

學習，不是把資訊塞進腦袋，
Learning isn't stuffing information into your head;
而是讓它慢慢變成你的一部分。
it's letting it slowly become a part of you.

學完一件事，試著寫下來；
After learning something, try writing it down;
寫下來之後，試著說給別人聽；
after writing it down, try explaining it to someone;
說給別人聽之後，試著用在生活裡。
after explaining it, try using it in your life.

✨ 真正學會一件事，是它改變了你的生活。
✨ You've truly learned something when it has changed your life.

今天不用再多看十篇文章，
Today, you don't need to read ten more articles;
只要挑一句最打動你的話，
just pick the one sentence that moves you most,
認真地，把它活出來。
and live it out, sincerely.
✨ 一句話活出來，勝過一百句話背下來。
✨ Living one sentence is worth more than memorizing a hundred.
今天就開始，不必等到準備好。
Start today; you don't have to wait until you're ready.
願你讀過的每一句話，都成為你的力量。📚
May every word you've read become your strength. 📚
