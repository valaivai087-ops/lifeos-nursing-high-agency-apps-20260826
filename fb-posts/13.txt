真正的自由，是有能力成為更好的自己。
True freedom is the ability to become a better version of yourself.

小時候以為，自由就是想做什麼就做什麼。
As children, we thought freedom meant doing whatever we wanted.
長大後才發現，
Only as adults do we discover
被慾望牽著走的時候，一點都不自由。
that being led by our cravings isn't freedom at all.

想睡卻放不下手機，不是自由；
Wanting to sleep but unable to put down the phone isn't freedom;
想改變卻一直拖延，不是自由；
Wanting to change but always procrastinating isn't freedom;
想離開卻被過去綁住，也不是自由。
Wanting to leave but being bound by the past isn't freedom either.

✨ Indulgence isn't freedom; it's just another kind of captivity.

真正的自由，
True freedom
是想早睡，就能放下手機；
is being able to put down your phone when you want to sleep;
是想改變，就能踏出第一步；
is being able to take the first step when you want to change;
