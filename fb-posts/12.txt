過去無法改變，但下一章由你來寫。
You can't change the past, but you can write the next chapter.

我們都有一段不想再提的過去，
We all have a past we'd rather not mention,
都有一個「都是因為他」的遺憾，
a regret that begins with "it's all because of them,"
都有一個深夜，覺得自己是全世界最委屈的人。
a late night when we felt like the most wronged person in the world.

那些傷害是真的，
Those wounds are real;
那些眼淚是真的，
those tears are real;
那些委屈也是真的。
that hurt is real, too.

但如果我們一直停在那一頁，
But if we keep staying on that page,
人生就只剩下那一頁。
our whole life becomes just that page.

✨ 你不只是那個受傷的人，你也是那個撐過來的人。
✨ You are not only the one who was hurt; you are also the one who survived.

故事怎麼寫，命運就怎麼走：
The way you write the story is the way your life unfolds:
寫成悲劇，你就一直在等待被拯救；
Write it as a tragedy, and you'll keep waiting to be rescued;
寫成抱怨，你就一直在原地打轉；
Write it as a complaint, and you'll keep going in circles;
寫成成長，你就會一直往前走。
Write it as growth, and you'll keep moving forward.

✨ 過去是用來學習的，不是用來居住的。
✨ The past is a place to learn from, not a place to live.

把筆拿回自己手上吧。
Take the pen back into your own hands.
你不必原諒所有人，
You don't have to forgive everyone,
但你可以選擇，不再讓過去決定你的未來。
but you can choose not to let the past decide your future.

✨ 每一個低谷，都是故事的轉折，不是結局。
✨ Every valley is a turning point in the story, not its ending.
接下來的故事，可以寫成一個關於勇氣和重生的故事。
The rest of your story can be one of courage and rebirth.
願你成為自己人生的主角。📖
May you become the main character of your own life. 📖
