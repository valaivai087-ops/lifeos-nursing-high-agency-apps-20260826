心亂的時候，先不要做重要的決定。
When your heart is in turmoil, don't make important decisions.

在最生氣的時候說出口的話，
Words spoken in the height of anger,
在最難過的時候做下的決定，
decisions made in the depths of sadness,
在最衝動的時候按下的送出，
messages sent in the heat of impulse,
往往都是事後最後悔的。
are often the ones we regret the most.

情緒來的時候，就像一場大浪，
When emotions come, they're like a great wave;
看不清方向，是很正常的。
not being able to see clearly is completely normal.

✨ 不要在憤怒時許下承諾，不要在難過時做出決定。
✨ Don't make promises in anger, and don't make decisions in sorrow.

先等一等吧：
So wait a little:
睡一晚，讓心沉澱；
sleep on it, and let your heart settle;
散個步，讓頭腦清醒；
take a walk, and let your mind clear;
跟信任的人聊一聊，讓自己多一個角度。
talk to someone you trust, and gain another point of view.

✨ 慢下來的決定，往往是最好的決定。
✨ Decisions made slowly are often the best ones.

把好處寫下來，
Write down the benefits,
把代價寫下來，
write down the costs,
再問自己一句：
and then ask yourself:
「一年後的我，會感謝現在的選擇嗎？」
"Will I, a year from now, be grateful for this choice?"

不急，不慌，不勉強。
No rush, no panic, no forcing.
✨ 心靜了，路就清楚了。
✨ When the heart is still, the path becomes clear.
✨ 你今天的每一個選擇，都在悄悄寫下你的未來。
✨ Every choice you make today is quietly writing your future.
等心靜下來了，你自然會知道，哪一條路才是對的。
Once your heart is calm, you'll know which road is right.
願你的每一個決定，都是清醒而溫柔的。🌙
May every decision you make be clear-minded and kind. 🌙
