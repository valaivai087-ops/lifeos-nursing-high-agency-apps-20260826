你把心放在哪裡，人生就開出什麼花。
Where you place your heart, there your life will bloom.

一天滑了幾百則動態，
You scroll through hundreds of posts a day;
看了別人的旅行，
You watch other people's travels,
看了別人的愛情，
You watch other people's love stories,
看了別人的成功，
You watch other people's success,
關掉手機的那一刻，心裡卻空空的。
Yet the moment you put the phone down, your heart feels empty.

不是你的人生不夠好，
It's not that your life isn't good enough;
是你太久沒有把目光，留給自己。
It's that you haven't looked at yourself in a long time.

注意力，是你最珍貴的能量：
Attention is your most precious energy:
放在比較上，你會越來越焦慮；
Spend it on comparison, and you grow more anxious;
放在抱怨上，你會越來越無力；
Spend it on complaining, and you grow more powerless;
放在感恩上，你會越來越富足；
Spend it on gratitude, and you grow richer inside;
放在成長上，你會越來越堅定。
Spend it on growth, and you grow more steady.

✨ 注意力放在哪裡，能量就流向哪裡。
✨ Where attention goes, energy flows.
✨ 你看什麼，就會變成什麼；
✨ What you look at, you become;
✨ 你在乎什麼，就會擁有什麼。
✨ What you care for, you will come to have.

今天，少滑十分鐘，多看一眼天空；
Today, scroll ten minutes less and look at the sky once more;
少比較一次，多感謝一次；
Compare once less, and give thanks once more;
少做十件小事，只做好三件重要的事。
Do ten small things less, and do three important things well.

你的人生，不在別人的動態裡，
Your life is not in other people's feeds;
而在你眼前的每一天。
It is in every day right in front of you.

✨ 把時間留給值得的人，把心留給重要的事。
✨ Save your time for the people who matter, and your heart for the things that matter.
願你的每一份專注，都開出一朵花。💛
May every bit of your focus bloom into a flower. 💛
