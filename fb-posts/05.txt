你擔心的事，大多都不會發生。
Most of what you worry about will never happen.

還沒開始，就先想好了最壞的結局；
Before you begin, you've already imagined the worst ending;
還沒見面，就先演完了被拒絕的戲；
Before you meet, you've already acted out the rejection;
還沒出發，就先害怕走錯了路。
Before you set out, you're already afraid of the wrong road.
還沒發生的事，就先讓自己難過了一百遍。
Things that haven't happened have already made you sad a hundred times.

我們總以為，先想到最糟，就不會受傷。
We think imagining the worst will keep us from getting hurt.
可是啊，那些擔心，往往比事情本身更讓人疲憊。
But those worries often exhaust us more than the events themselves.

✨ 焦慮不是預言，只是心在害怕；
✨ Anxiety is not a prophecy; it's only the heart being afraid.
✨ 擔心不會改變明天，只會偷走今天。
✨ Worry won't change tomorrow; it only steals today.

未來不是你害怕的樣子，
The future is not what you fear it to be;
未來不是別人預言的樣子，
The future is not what others predict it to be;
未來是你今天，一步一步走出來的樣子。
The future is what you build, step by step, today.
✨ 明天的路，是今天的腳印鋪成的。
✨ Tomorrow's road is paved with today's footsteps.

所以，用事實代替想像，
So replace imagination with facts,
用行動代替擔心，
Replace worry with action,
用一個小小的成功，代替一整晚的失眠。
Replace a whole sleepless night with one small success.

✨ 最壞的事還沒發生，最好的事也還沒發生；
✨ The worst hasn't happened yet — and neither has the best;
✨ 你今天怎麼走，決定明天遇見哪一個。
✨ How you walk today decides which one you'll meet tomorrow.

深呼吸，你現在是安全的。
Breathe deeply — right now, you are safe.
放過還沒發生的明天，
Let go of the tomorrow that hasn't come,
好好過眼前的今天就好。
And simply live today well.
願你心安，願你無懼。🌤
May your heart be at peace, and may you be unafraid. 🌤
