你對自己說的話，會慢慢變成你的人生。
The words you say to yourself slowly become your life.

「我好笨。」
"I'm so stupid."
「我什麼都做不好。」
"I can't do anything right."
「我又搞砸了。」
"I messed up again."

如果有一個朋友，每天這樣跟你說話，
If a friend spoke to you like this every day,
你大概早就離開他了吧。
you'd probably have walked away long ago.

那為什麼，我們對自己這麼殘忍呢？
So why are we so cruel to ourselves?

✨ 語言是種子，你說什麼，心裡就長出什麼。
✨ Words are seeds; whatever you say is what grows in your heart.

說「我不行」，你就真的越來越沒力氣；
Say "I can't," and you really lose strength;
說「我好累」，你就真的越來越疲憊；
Say "I'm so tired," and you really grow more weary;
說「我正在學」，你就真的越來越有勇氣。
Say "I'm learning," and you really grow more courageous.

從今天開始，試著像對待好朋友一樣，對自己說話：
Starting today, speak to yourself the way you'd speak to a good friend:
「沒關係，你已經很努力了。」
"It's okay, you've already tried so hard."
「這次沒做好，下次會更好。」
"It didn't go well this time; next time will be better."
「我為你感到驕傲。」
"I'm proud of you."

✨ 抱怨會讓路越走越窄，感謝會讓路越走越寬。
✨ Complaining narrows the road; gratitude widens it.
✨ 改變你說的話，就能改變你的心；
✨ Change your words, and you change your heart;
✨ 改變你的心，就能改變你的人生。
✨ Change your heart, and you change your life.

你值得被溫柔對待，
You deserve to be treated gently,
尤其是被你自己。
especially by yourself.
願你對自己說的每一句話，都像擁抱。💗
May every word you say to yourself feel like a hug. 💗
