你怎麼過一天，就怎麼過一生。
How you spend your days is how you spend your life.

「等我有空再說吧。」
"I'll do it when I have time."
「等忙完這陣子。」
"Once this busy season is over."
「等以後再說。」
"Maybe later."

我們總在等，
We're always waiting,
等著等著，一年又過去了。
and while we wait, another year slips by.

✨ 時間從來不會多出來，它只會被我們交出去。
✨ Time never appears out of nowhere; it only gets given away.

交給手機，一天就碎了；
Give it to your phone, and the day falls to pieces;
交給瑣事，一天就散了；
Give it to trivial things, and the day scatters;
交給焦慮，一天就沒了。
Give it to anxiety, and the day is gone.

✨ 你的時間在哪裡，你的人生就在哪裡。
✨ Where your time is, there your life is.

今天，為自己留一段完整的時間吧：
Today, save a whole block of time for yourself:
陪家人好好吃一頓飯，
share a real meal with your family,
安靜地讀幾頁書，
quietly read a few pages,
認真地做一件重要的事，
focus fully on one important thing,
或者，什麼都不做，好好地發個呆。
or do nothing at all, and simply let your mind wander.

睡前一小時，把手機放遠一點；
An hour before bed, put your phone farther away;
早上第一小時，把心留給自己。
The first hour of the morning, keep your heart for yourself.

✨ 珍惜時間，就是珍惜生命。
✨ To cherish time is to cherish life.
✨ 不要等到有空才生活，生活就在每一個現在。
✨ Don't wait until you're free to live; life is in every present moment.
願你的每一天，都過得踏實而溫暖。⏳
May every one of your days be grounded and warm. ⏳
