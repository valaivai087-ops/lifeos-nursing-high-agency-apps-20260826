情緒不是敵人，是心在輕輕敲門。
Emotions are not enemies; they are your heart gently knocking.

難過的時候，你是不是總說「我沒事」？
When you're sad, do you always say "I'm fine"?
生氣的時候，你是不是總告訴自己「要忍」？
When you're angry, do you always tell yourself "just bear it"?
委屈的時候，你是不是總假裝「不在乎」？
When you're hurt, do you always pretend "it doesn't matter"?

可是被壓下去的情緒，不會消失，
But emotions pushed down don't disappear;
它只會躲起來，
they only hide,
在某個深夜，讓你莫名地崩潰。
and one late night, they make you fall apart for no clear reason.

✨ 情緒不是用來壓的，是用來聽的。
✨ Emotions aren't meant to be suppressed; they're meant to be heard.

難過，是在告訴你：你失去了重要的東西；
Sadness tells you: you've lost something important;
生氣，是在告訴你：你的界線被踩到了；
Anger tells you: your boundary has been crossed;
焦慮，是在告訴你：你很在乎這件事；
Anxiety tells you: you care deeply about this;
委屈，是在告訴你：你值得被好好對待。
Hurt tells you: you deserve to be treated well.

親愛的，想哭就哭吧。
Dear one, if you want to cry, cry.
✨ 哭不是軟弱，是心在替你卸下重量。
✨ Crying isn't weakness; it's your heart setting down its burden.

寫下來，讓情緒有地方去；
Write it down, so your feelings have somewhere to go;
深呼吸，讓身體慢下來；
Breathe deeply, so your body can slow down;
找個人說說話，讓心不再孤單。
Talk to someone, so your heart isn't alone.

✨ 允許自己難過，才是真正的堅強。
✨ Allowing yourself to be sad is true strength.
✨ 情緒流過你，而不是困住你，你就自由了。
✨ When emotions flow through you instead of trapping you, you are free.
你的感受很重要，你也很重要。
Your feelings matter, and so do you.
不管今天有多難，情緒都會過去，天總會晴。
However hard today is, the feelings will pass and the sky will clear.
願你的每一場雨，都有停的時候。🌧️🌤
May every rain in your heart find its end. 🌧️🌤
