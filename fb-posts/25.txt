換一片土壤，花也能開得很美。
In different soil, a flower can bloom beautifully.

有些地方，一待就讓人心累；
Some places drain your heart the moment you arrive;
有些人，一靠近就讓人洩氣；
Some people deflate you the moment you're near them;
有些圈子，一融入就讓人忘了自己。
Some circles make you forget yourself the moment you join.

不是你不夠好，
It's not that you aren't good enough;
可能只是你待錯了地方。
you may simply be in the wrong place.

✨ 你是什麼樣的人，和你待在什麼樣的地方，有很大的關係。
✨ Who you become has a lot to do with where you choose to stay.

跟抱怨的人在一起，你會越來越灰心；
Spend time with complainers, and you'll grow discouraged;
跟努力的人在一起，你會越來越有力量；
Spend time with hard workers, and you'll grow stronger;
跟溫暖的人在一起，你會越來越柔軟。
Spend time with warm people, and you'll grow softer.

✨ 近朱者赤，近光者亮。
✨ Stay close to light, and you will shine.

今天，先整理一個小抽屜，
Today, start by tidying one small drawer,
讓陽光照進房間；
and let the sunlight into your room;
布置一個讓你想看書的角落，
set up a little corner where you want to read,
讓生活有一點秩序。
and give your life a little order.

靠近那些讓你想變好的人，
Draw near to those who make you want to be better,
遠離那些一直消耗你的關係。
and step away from relationships that keep draining you.

你值得待在一個讓你發光的地方。
You deserve to be in a place that lets you shine.
好的環境不會自己出現，但你可以慢慢把它布置出來。
A good environment won't appear on its own, but you can slowly create it.
✨ 人會被環境改變，也可以親手改變環境。
✨ Environments change people, and people can change their environments too.
✨ 改變不了自己的時候，就先改變環境。
✨ When you can't change yourself, change your surroundings first.
願你身邊都是好人，眼前都是好風景。🌻
May you be surrounded by good people and beautiful views. 🌻
