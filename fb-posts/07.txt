你不是不夠好，你只是還在路上。
You are not "not good enough" — you are still on the way.

「我就是這麼差。」
"I'm just this bad."
「我這輩子就這樣了。」
"This is all my life will ever be."
「我不可能做到的。」
"There's no way I can do it."

這些話，你是不是在心裡說過好多遍？
Have you said these words to yourself many times?

說久了，就信了；
Say them long enough, and you believe them;
信久了，就不敢再試了；
Believe them long enough, and you stop trying;
不試了，就真的變成那樣了。
Stop trying, and they really come true.

✨ 你相信什麼，就會活成什麼。
✨ What you believe, you become.

可是親愛的，
But dear one,
花不會因為還沒開，就不是花；
A flower is still a flower before it blooms;
樹不會因為還沒長高，就不是樹；
A tree is still a tree before it grows tall;
你不會因為還沒成功，就不值得。
And you are still worthy before you succeed.

✨ 限制你的從來不是能力，而是你對自己的看法。
✨ What limits you is never your ability, but the way you see yourself.

今天，換幾句話對自己說吧：
Today, try saying different words to yourself:
把「我不行」，換成「我還沒學會」；
Replace "I can't" with "I haven't learned it yet";
把「我好差」，換成「我正在進步」；
Replace "I'm so bad" with "I'm getting better";
把「不可能」，換成「試試看」。
Replace "impossible" with "let's try."

每一次嘗試，都是在對自己說：「我值得。」
Every attempt is a way of telling yourself: "I am worth it."
你比你以為的，更有力量。
You are stronger than you think.
✨ 你不必很厲害才能開始，但你必須開始才能變得厲害。
✨ You don't have to be great to start, but you have to start to become great.
願你相信自己，像相信明天會天亮一樣。🌸
May you believe in yourself the way you believe the sun will rise tomorrow. 🌸
