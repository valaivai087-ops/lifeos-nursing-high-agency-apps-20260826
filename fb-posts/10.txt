真正讓人生變好的事，往往不是立刻快樂的事。
What truly makes life better is rarely what makes you happy right away.

追劇到半夜很快樂，
Binge-watching until midnight feels good,
可是隔天醒來好疲憊；
but you wake up exhausted;
亂買東西很快樂，
Shopping on impulse feels good,
可是月底看著帳單好心慌；
but the bill at the end of the month makes you anxious;
吃宵夜很快樂，
A late-night snack feels good,
可是鏡子裡的自己越來越陌生。
but the person in the mirror feels more like a stranger.

✨ 短暫的快樂像煙火，燦爛一下就散了；
✨ Short-lived pleasure is like fireworks — brilliant, then gone;
✨ 長久的幸福像大樹，要慢慢地長。
✨ Lasting happiness is like a tree — it grows slowly.

那些真正讓生命豐盛的事：
The things that truly enrich life:
讀一本書，
reading a book,
走一段路，
taking a walk,
存一點錢，
saving a little money,
陪一個人，
spending time with someone,
一開始都不刺激，甚至有點辛苦。
are not exciting at first — even a little hard.

但時間會記得，
But time remembers
你為自己種下的每一顆種子。
every seed you plant for yourself.

✨ 今天的忍耐，是明天的禮物；
✨ Today's patience is tomorrow's gift;
✨ 今天的累積，是未來的底氣。
✨ Today's small steps are tomorrow's strength.

少一點「現在就要」，
A little less "I want it now,"
多一點「慢慢會好」。
a little more "it will get better, slowly."

✨ 人生最好的投資，是投資在會越來越好的事情上。
✨ The best investment in life is in things that grow better over time.
願你擁有等待花開的耐心。🌳
May you have the patience to wait for the flowers to bloom. 🌳
