你已經很安全了，可以把盔甲放下了。
You are safe now; you can take off your armor.

有些人，從小就得很堅強。
Some people had to be strong from a very young age.
要察言觀色，
They learned to read the room,
要事事小心，
to be careful about everything,
要把自己的需要，藏得好好的。
to hide their own needs well.

不敢放鬆，
They never dared to relax,
因為怕一放鬆，壞事就會發生。
afraid that if they did, something bad would happen.

長大以後，那副盔甲一直穿在身上，
As adults, that armor stayed on;
好重，好累，
so heavy, so tiring,
連笑的時候，都帶著防備。
that even their smiles carried a guard.

✨ 盔甲保護過你，但也讓你感受不到擁抱。
✨ Armor once protected you, but it also keeps you from feeling embraced.

親愛的，你已經不是那個無助的孩子了。
Dear one, you are no longer that helpless child.
現在的你，有能力保護自己；
Now you are able to protect yourself;
現在的你，有權利說不；
now you have the right to say no;
現在的你，可以選擇讓你安心的人。
now you can choose people who make you feel safe.

✨ 真正的勇敢，不是一直武裝自己，
✨ True courage isn't always arming yourself;
✨ 而是敢讓自己柔軟。
✨ it's daring to let yourself be soft.

今天，允許自己鬆一口氣。
Today, allow yourself to breathe out.
去曬曬太陽，去走進大自然，
Go sit in the sun, go walk in nature,
去做一件純粹因為喜歡而做的事。
go do something simply because you love it.

✨ 放下防備，你才會發現，世界其實有很多善意。
✨ Put down your guard, and you'll find the world holds a lot of kindness.
願那顆一直緊繃的心，終於可以好好休息。🌿
May your ever-tense heart finally get to rest. 🌿
