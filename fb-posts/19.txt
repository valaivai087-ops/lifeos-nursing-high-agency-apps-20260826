慢一點沒關係，只要方向是對的。
It's okay to go slowly, as long as you're heading the right way.

你每天都好忙好忙，
You're so busy every day,
忙著回訊息，
busy replying to messages,
忙著處理瑣事，
busy handling small things,
忙著滿足每個人的期待，
busy meeting everyone's expectations,
卻好像什麼都沒有改變。
and yet nothing seems to change.

✨ 忙碌不等於前進，就像原地跑步永遠到不了遠方。
✨ Being busy isn't the same as moving forward, just as running in place never gets you anywhere.

有時候，我們需要的不是更努力，
Sometimes what we need isn't to try harder,
而是停下來想一想：
but to stop and think:
「哪一件事，才是真正重要的？」
"Which thing really matters?"

每天只做好三件重要的事，
Do just three important things well each day,
其他的，就放過自己吧。
and let yourself off the hook for the rest.

先完成，再完美；
Finish first, perfect later;
先開始，再調整；
Start first, adjust later;
先走一步，再看下一步。
Take one step, then look for the next.

✨ 不是做得多才叫努力，走對方向才是真正的前進。
✨ Doing more isn't what makes effort count; heading the right way is what moves you forward.
✨ 一次只做一件事，是最溫柔的高效率。
✨ Doing one thing at a time is the gentlest kind of efficiency.

慢一點沒關係，
It's okay to be slow;
烏龜也能到達終點，
the tortoise reaches the finish line too;
重要的是，你一直在路上。
what matters is that you keep going.

✨ 每天前進一小步，一年後就是一大步。
✨ One small step a day becomes a giant leap in a year.
願你忙得有方向，走得有力量。🚶
May your busyness have direction, and your steps have strength. 🚶
