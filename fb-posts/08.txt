人生不是只有對與錯，還有很多溫柔的灰色。
Life isn't only right and wrong; there are many gentle shades of grey.

年輕的時候，我們總想把世界分得清清楚楚：
When we were young, we wanted to divide the world clearly:
好人和壞人，
good people and bad people,
成功和失敗，
success and failure,
愛和不愛。
love and not love.

走著走著才明白，
Only after walking a long way do we understand
很多事，沒有絕對的答案。
that many things have no absolute answer.

那個傷害你的人，也許也曾被深深傷害；
The one who hurt you may once have been deeply hurt;
那段失敗的經歷，也許正是你成長的開始；
That failure may have been the beginning of your growth;
那場不想要的離別，也許是為了更好的相遇。
That unwanted goodbye may have made room for a better meeting.

✨ 世界不是非黑即白，而是一幅慢慢展開的畫。
✨ The world is not black and white; it is a painting slowly unfolding.
✨ 看得越深，心就越寬；
✨ The deeper you see, the wider your heart;
✨ 心越寬，路就越多。
✨ The wider your heart, the more paths appear.

當你不再急著下結論，
When you stop rushing to conclusions,
你會多了一份理解；
you gain understanding;
當你不再急著分對錯，
when you stop rushing to judge right and wrong,
你會多了一份慈悲；
you gain compassion;
當你願意用三個角度看同一件事，
when you're willing to see one thing from three angles,
你會多了一份智慧。
you gain wisdom.

✨ 成熟，不是看透了世界，而是看懂了人心。
✨ Maturity isn't seeing through the world, but understanding the human heart.
願你眼中有是非，心中有溫柔。🌈
May you see right from wrong, and still hold tenderness in your heart. 🌈
