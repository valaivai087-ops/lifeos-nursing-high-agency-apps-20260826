過去的傷，不必成為未來的路。
Past wounds need not become your future path.

被傷過的人，總會特別小心。
Those who have been hurt become especially careful.
一點風吹草動，就以為暴風雨又要來了；
At the slightest rustle, they think another storm is coming;
一句無心的話，就以為又要被丟下了；
At a careless word, they think they'll be left behind again;
一個小小的失誤，就以為自己又要失敗了。
At a small mistake, they think they're about to fail once more.

這不是你太敏感，
This isn't you being too sensitive;
是你的心，太努力想保護你。
It's your heart trying so hard to protect you.

可是親愛的，那張寫滿傷痕的舊地圖，
But dear one, that old map covered in scars
已經不適合現在的你了。
no longer fits who you are now.

你已經長大了，
You have grown up;
你已經有能力照顧自己了，
You are now able to take care of yourself;
你已經不是那個無助的孩子了。
You are no longer that helpless child.

✨ 記憶會告訴你哪裡曾經痛過，
✨ Memory tells you where it once hurt,
✨ 但不能決定你以後要往哪裡走。
✨ But it cannot decide where you will go next.
✨ 傷口會結痂，心也會重新學會相信。
✨ Wounds heal over, and the heart learns to trust again.

從今天起，替自己收集一些溫暖的片刻：
Starting today, collect some warm moments for yourself:
一句被理解的話，一頓好好吃的飯，
A word of understanding, a good meal,
一次小小的成功，一個安心的擁抱。
A small success, a reassuring hug.

慢慢地，心會重新畫出一張新的地圖。
Slowly, your heart will draw a brand-new map.
✨ 舊地圖陪你走過黑夜，新地圖帶你走向黎明。
✨ The old map carried you through the night; the new one will lead you to dawn.
你值得一個不再害怕的明天。
You deserve a tomorrow without fear.
願你被溫柔治癒，也學會溫柔地治癒自己。🌱
May you be gently healed — and learn to heal yourself gently. 🌱
