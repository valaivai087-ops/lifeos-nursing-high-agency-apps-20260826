生活不必完美，只要記得常常整理。
Life doesn't need to be perfect; just remember to tidy it often.

房間久了會亂，
A room gets messy over time;
行程久了會亂，
a schedule gets messy over time;
心，久了也會亂。
and the heart, too, gets messy over time.

越想把一切都抓在手裡，
The more you try to hold everything in your hands,
越會發現，什麼都抓不住。
the more you find you can hold nothing at all.

✨ 控制得越多，失控得越快；放下一點，反而更穩。
✨ The more you control, the faster things spin out; let go a little, and you steady.

人生其實不需要完美的控制，
Life doesn't need perfect control;
只需要一點規律，
it just needs a little rhythm,
一點整理，
a little tidying,
一點放手。
a little letting go.

✨ 簡單的生活，裝得下最多的幸福。
✨ A simple life has room for the most happiness.

像定期打掃房間一樣，
Just as you clean your room regularly,
也記得常常整理自己的心：
remember to tidy your heart often too:
丟掉用不到的東西，
throw away what you no longer use,
放下帶不走的煩惱，
let go of worries you can't carry,
留下真正重要的人和事。
and keep the people and things that truly matter.

✨ 不更新的東西會慢慢變亂，不整理的心會慢慢變重。
✨ What isn't renewed slowly falls into disorder; a heart left untidied slowly grows heavy.

不求一次改變全部，
Don't try to change everything at once;
一次一小步，就很好了。
one small step at a time is enough.
願你的生活，簡單、清爽、有餘裕。🍵
May your life be simple, fresh, and full of breathing room. 🍵
