放不下沒關係，慢慢來就好。
It's okay if you can't let go yet — take your time.

有些人，明明已經離開很久了，
Some people left long ago,
想起來，還是會痛；
yet thinking of them still hurts;
有些事，明明已經過去很久了，
Some things ended long ago,
夜深人靜時，還是一遍遍重播；
yet late at night they replay again and again;
有些話，明明已經說不出口了，
Some words can no longer be spoken,
卻還一直卡在心裡。
yet they're still stuck in your heart.

不是你不夠堅強，
It's not that you aren't strong enough;
是你曾經真心付出過。
it's that you once gave your whole heart.

✨ 放不下，是因為你曾經那麼認真地愛過。
✨ You can't let go because you once loved so sincerely.

不用逼自己馬上忘記，
You don't have to force yourself to forget right away;
不用逼自己假裝不痛，
You don't have to pretend it doesn't hurt;
不用逼自己一定要原諒。
You don't have to force yourself to forgive.

只要在往前走的路上，
Just keep walking forward,
多創造一些新的、美好的回憶：
and create some new, beautiful memories along the way:
一趟小旅行，一個新朋友，一個新的開始。
a small trip, a new friend, a new beginning.

✨ 時間不會帶走記憶，但會帶走刺痛。
✨ Time doesn't take away memories, but it takes away the sting.
✨ 新的回憶，會溫柔地蓋住舊的傷口。
✨ New memories gently cover old wounds.

有一天你會發現，
One day you'll notice
那段過去還在，但它已經不會再刺痛你了。
that the past is still there, but it no longer hurts you.

✨ 記憶會影響你，但不會定義你。
✨ Memories may influence you, but they don't define you.
願你把回憶留在心裡，把腳步留給未來。🍂
May you keep the memories in your heart, and your steps for the future. 🍂
