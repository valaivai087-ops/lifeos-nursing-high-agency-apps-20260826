最好的安全感，是自己給自己的。
The best sense of security is the one you give yourself.

我們總在找一個人，希望他能給我們安全感；
We keep looking for someone to give us security;
我們總在找一份工作，希望它能讓我們安心；
We keep looking for a job that will make us feel safe;
我們總在找一個地方，希望它能讓我們依靠。
We keep looking for a place we can lean on.

可是人會離開，
But people leave;
工作會變動，
jobs change;
世界從來都不是永遠穩定的。
and the world is never stable forever.

✨ 把安全感交給別人，就等於把心交給風。
✨ Handing your security to others is like handing your heart to the wind.

真正的安全感，
True security
是早睡早起的規律，
is the rhythm of sleeping and waking on time;
是存了一點點錢的踏實，
is the steadiness of having a little money saved;
是身體健康的安穩，
is the calm of a healthy body;
是一個人的時候，也能好好照顧自己。
is being able to take care of yourself when you're alone.

✨ 你若盛開，清風自來；
✨ When you bloom, the gentle breeze will come;
✨ 你若安穩，世界也會跟著安穩。
✨ When you are steady, the world steadies with you.

慢慢來，把生活過穩：
Take it slow, and steady your life:
一日三餐好好吃，
eat three proper meals a day,
一週幾次好好動，
move your body a few times a week,
每天晚上好好睡。
and sleep well every night.

✨ 你就是自己最堅固的港灣。
✨ You are your own safest harbor.
願你不必依靠誰，也能活得很安心。⚓
May you live with peace of mind, without having to depend on anyone. ⚓
