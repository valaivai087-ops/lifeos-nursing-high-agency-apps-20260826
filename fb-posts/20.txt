失敗不是終點，是生命提醒你轉個彎。
Failure isn't the end; it's life reminding you to turn.

考試沒考好，
You didn't do well on the exam;
面試沒上，
you didn't get the job;
感情走散了，
the relationship fell apart;
努力了很久，結果還是不如預期。
you worked so hard, and it still didn't turn out as hoped.

你是不是在心裡跟自己說：
Did you tell yourself, deep inside,
「我果然不行」？
"I knew I wasn't good enough"?

可是親愛的，
But dear one,
失敗不是在否定你這個人，
failure isn't a verdict on who you are;
它只是在告訴你：這條路，需要調整一下。
it's only telling you: this path needs some adjusting.

✨ 失敗不是你不好，是你正在變好的路上。
✨ Failure doesn't mean you're not good; it means you're on the way to getting better.

每一次跌倒，都在教你站得更穩；
Every fall teaches you to stand more firmly;
每一次錯誤，都在教你看得更清；
Every mistake teaches you to see more clearly;
每一次放棄，都在教你更懂自己要什麼。
Every time you give up teaches you more about what you really want.

✨ 把失敗當成老師，不要當成法官。
✨ Treat failure as a teacher, not a judge.

試著每週問自己三個問題：
Try asking yourself three questions each week:
這次，我做對了什麼？
What did I do right this time?
這次，我可以怎麼調整？
What can I adjust this time?
下次，我想怎麼做得更好？
How do I want to do better next time?

✨ 成功不是從不跌倒，而是每次跌倒都願意再站起來。
✨ Success isn't never falling; it's being willing to rise every time you fall.
願你越跌越勇，越走越穩。🌱
May every fall make you braver, and every step make you steadier. 🌱
