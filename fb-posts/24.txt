好的關係，是讓彼此都能好好做自己。
A good relationship lets both people truly be themselves.

愛一個人，不是把他緊緊抓住；
Loving someone isn't holding them tightly;
愛一個人，不是委屈自己去換他留下來；
Loving someone isn't sacrificing yourself to make them stay;
愛一個人，不是讓自己越來越不像自己。
Loving someone isn't becoming less and less like yourself.

如果一段關係，讓你越來越累，
If a relationship makes you more and more tired,
越來越不安，
more and more insecure,
越來越小心翼翼，
more and more cautious,
那也許不是愛，是害怕失去。
then maybe it isn't love — it's fear of losing.

✨ 抓得越緊，越容易失去；給得剛好，才能長久。
✨ The tighter you hold, the easier it is to lose; give just enough, and it can last.

親愛的，你值得被好好地愛——
Dear one, you deserve to be loved well —
不必討好，
without people-pleasing,
不必證明，
without proving yourself,
不必委屈，
without swallowing your hurt,
只因為你是你。
simply because you are you.

✨ 真正愛你的人，不會讓你一直猜。
✨ Someone who truly loves you won't keep you guessing.

學會溫柔地說「不」，
Learn to gently say "no,"
學會誠實地說出需要，
learn to honestly say what you need,
學會離開消耗你的人，
learn to walk away from people who drain you,
留下那些讓你安心的人。
and keep those who make you feel at peace.

✨ 好的關係，是兩個人都變得更好。
✨ A good relationship makes both people better.
願你被愛，也懂得好好愛人。💞
May you be loved, and know how to love well. 💞
