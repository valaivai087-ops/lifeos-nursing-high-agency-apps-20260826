你不是「就這樣」，你一直在長成更好的樣子。
You are not "just like this" — you are always growing into someone better.

「我就是這種個性。」
"That's just my personality."
「我天生就不行。」
"I was born this way; I just can't."
「我改不了了。」
"I can't change anymore."

這些標籤，是別人給你的，
Were these labels given to you by others,
還是你自己，早就習慣了貼在身上？
or have you simply grown used to wearing them?

✨ 人不是一張定型的照片，而是一部還在拍攝的電影。
✨ A person is not a finished photograph, but a film still being shot.

昨天的你，不代表今天的你；
Who you were yesterday doesn't define who you are today;
今天的你，不代表明天的你；
Who you are today doesn't define who you'll be tomorrow;
你每天的選擇，都在悄悄改寫你是誰。
Every choice you make each day quietly rewrites who you are.

✨ 你不是被過去定義的，你是被每一個今天塑造的。
✨ You are not defined by your past; you are shaped by every today.

今天的你，可以比昨天多勇敢一點點；
Today, you can be a little braver than yesterday;
可以比昨天多溫柔一點點；
a little gentler than yesterday;
可以比昨天多相信自己一點點。
a little more trusting of yourself than yesterday.

不用一下子變成另一個人，
You don't have to become someone else overnight;
只要每天，做一件「更好的自己」會做的小事。
just do one small thing each day that "a better you" would do.

每天早上問自己一句：
Every morning, ask yourself:
「今天，我想成為怎樣的人？」
"Who do I want to be today?"

你可以溫柔，也可以勇敢；你可以慢，但不要停。
You can be gentle and brave; you can be slow, but don't stop.
✨ 你是誰，由你決定。
✨ Who you are is yours to decide.
✨ 毛毛蟲不會永遠是毛毛蟲，你也不會永遠停在這裡。
✨ A caterpillar isn't a caterpillar forever, and you won't stay here forever.
願你一天一天，長成自己喜歡的樣子。🦋
May you grow, day by day, into someone you love to be. 🦋
